# CV jury rubric (fixed — do not change between rounds)

Candidate context (true, from the candidate): fresh S.Kom. graduate (Indonesia, 2026), currently 6 months
into a full-time Full-Stack Systems Engineer (AI & Automation) role. Target: "Full-Stack AI Engineer" roles —
full-stack web, ERP/business systems, ML/LLM systems, automation. Applying in Indonesia and remote/international.

You are judging ONLY the documents given. Do not reward claims you can't see; penalise vague, unverifiable or
inflated wording. Be strict: a 9 means "would forward to a hiring manager immediately and the doc has no
real flaw left"; a 10 means "best-in-class for a candidate at this level — nothing to change".

Score each criterion 0–10, then give an overall 0–10 (not necessarily the mean; your honest judgement).

1. Positioning — in 6 seconds, is it clear who this is, what role, and why them?
2. Impact & evidence — concrete outcomes, numbers, scope; not duty lists.
3. Relevance & prioritisation — strongest, most relevant material first; filler cut.
4. Writing — concise, active, specific, consistent tense and punctuation, no fluff.
5. Visual design & scannability — hierarchy, whitespace, alignment, 1-page density, consistency.
6. ATS-safety — single column, standard headings, parseable text, contact info, keywords.
7. Credibility — does anything look inflated, inconsistent (dates/titles) or suspicious?

Output format (exactly):
SCORES: positioning=X impact=X relevance=X writing=X design=X ats=X credibility=X OVERALL=X
TOP_FIXES: up to 6 bullets, most important first, each concrete and actionable (quote the line).
WHAT_WORKS: up to 3 bullets.
